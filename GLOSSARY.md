# TSDM forum workspace

The workspace presents the forum's boards, threads and posts while preserving their meaning across reading and authoring.

## Language

**Board**:
A forum area that contains threads and may contain subordinate boards.
_Avoid_: Channel

**Thread**:
A discussion in a board, consisting of an opening post and its replies.
_Avoid_: Chat, conversation

**Post**:
A contribution within a thread, including the opening post or a reply.
_Avoid_: Message, bubble

**Forum draft**:
An unpublished thread saved by the forum for its author to resume or publish.
_Avoid_: Unfinished writing, unsent reply

**Writing context**:
A composition in progress together with its authoring action, destination, content and options.
_Avoid_: Forum draft

**Thread price**:
The forum-credit charge set by an author for access to a thread's paid content.

**Read permission**:
A forum-defined requirement a member must meet to read a thread.

**Red packet (hongbao)**:
A pool of forum credits offered for eligible members to claim, with configured distribution and claim conditions.
